# What is Machine Learning ? 

> "Machine Learning is the study field that gives the computers the ability to learn without being explicitly programmed" -Arthur Samuel (1959)

Arthur Samuel is the programmer of the checkers game in computer, even though he was not a very good checkers player. He programmed the computer to play tens of thousands of games against itself, seeing the moves or board positions that lead to winning games and bord positions that lead to loosing, the computer eventually became better and better at the game. 

- The more opportunities you give your model to learn, the better it will perform.

* Machine Learning Algorithms : 
    - Supervised learning (used in most real world applications)
    - Unsupervised learning 
    - Recommender Systems
    - Reinforcement Learning
